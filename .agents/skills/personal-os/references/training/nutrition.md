# Nutrition log

Record what was eaten, approximate portions, and the source of any nutrition estimate. Leave unknown quantities unknown. Do not treat a food suggestion or a reported habit as a meal.

Use consistent measurement conditions to compare weight over time. A single measurement or partially logged day does not establish an energy surplus or trend. General protein estimates can use the person's reported body weight; label targets separately from intake. Use dietary constraints the person actually reported.
